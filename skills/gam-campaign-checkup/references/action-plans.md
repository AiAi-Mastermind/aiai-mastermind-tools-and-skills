# Action Plans — Brown Belt and CEO Cadence

## The one-move rule

“My #1 Action Item This Week — pick ONE thing... Don't try to fix everything. Pick the highest-impact item.”

One move means one accountable outcome, not a list joined by “and.” Prefer reversible changes. Pause rather than delete. The member executes every account change manually.

## Workbook Round 4 menu

Choose one item exactly from this menu when it fits the diagnosis:

- Add negative keywords for red flag search terms found in Round 3.
- Improve ad strength to Excellent (add headlines with my state/city).
- Set up marketing source tracking in my CRM / commission tracker.
- Have the WIIFM conversation with my team about asking “How did you hear about us?”
- Upload my agency photos to my ad (if running 30+ days).
- Set up my monthly tracking spreadsheet (the 15-minute CEO review).

A Green campaign does not need manufactured optimization. “Leave the campaign alone and complete the month-end review” is valid.

## Commitment format

Always end the plan with:

> I will **[one action]** by **[date]**; I'll bring the result to the Wednesday Huddle on **[date]**.

If no calendar date is supplied, ask for one or propose the next practical date and mark it for confirmation. Huddle: `aiaimastermind.com/adsmasteryzoom`.

## Select the one action

Use this priority:

1. **Missing verdict data:** pull/filter the agency website report with matching dates.
2. **Missing business outcome:** set up or repair Google Ads source tracking in CRM/commission tracker.
3. **Meaningful Red search-term spend:** add the highest-impact clear Red term as a negative or apply the missing master list.
4. **Clean terms but weak ad quality:** improve Ad Strength toward Excellent with state/city headlines, or upload agency photos after 30+ days.
5. **Clean terms and good ad quality but CPC/outcomes off:** use one bid/mode move selected from the diagnosis playbook and Huddle guidance.
6. **Green:** leave it alone, monitor, or improve tracking.

Do not skip ahead. “Check search terms first. Then ad quality. Then bids.”

## Monthly 15-minute CEO review

At month end:

1. Set and write the review date range.
2. Pull Google Ads spend and 3-min+ ad-call conversions.
3. Pull the agency website report for exact same dates.
4. Filter to the campaign tracking URL.
5. Record page views, quote starts, and phone clicks.
6. Calculate Total Leads and CPQ.
7. Pull households closed from CRM/commission tracker.
8. Calculate CPA and compare with the member's own LTV.
9. Assign Green, Yellow, or Red using the exact rule.
10. If Yellow/Red, inspect search terms first.
11. Record one action only.
12. Schedule the next review.

Remember the lag: households may close after the lead month. Note cohort/date context rather than forcing a false same-month conclusion.

## Belt self-assessment

### White Belt — Find data

**You are here when:** locating spend, conversions, campaign report, and tracking URL.

**Earn Blue by:** producing matching-date spend + conversions + quote starts + phone clicks without guessing.

### Blue Belt — Know numbers

**You are here when:** calculating Total Leads, CPQ, CPA, and comparing CPA with LTV.

**Earn Purple by:** correctly applying Signal Light and explaining why.

### Purple Belt — Diagnose

**You are here when:** reading search terms, marking Green/Yellow/Red, totaling junk spend, verifying four exclusion lists are applied, then checking ad quality and Auction Insights.

**Earn Brown by:** choosing one evidence-based, reversible move instead of a laundry list.

### Brown Belt — Tune

**You are here when:** executing one move, documenting date, waiting the correct period, and comparing the same scoreboard afterward.

**Earn Black by:** maintaining monthly cadence, protecting Green campaigns, and deciding from CPA versus LTV.

### Black Belt — Run like a CEO

**You are here when:** answering “Is it working?” in under 60 seconds with the complete scoreboard, delegating clean data collection, and spending attention where it has highest return.

## When to come back

- **14 days after a match-type switch.** Expect clicks to drop 40–70% and CPC to rise; judge CPQ after the wait.
- **30 days for a Month 1 campaign.** Google is learning; use Yellow unless an obvious red flag appears.
- **Monthly otherwise.** Complete the 15-minute CEO review at month end.
- **Immediately at the Wednesday Huddle** for an installation/template decision, restart, geography restructuring, or unsupported question.
